Where Ancient Wisdom Meets Modern Science

YogaXBiofeedback pioneers the scientific validation of traditional Indian breathing practices through cutting-edge biofeedback technology.

Founded at the prestigious IIT Mandi IKSMHA Centre, we bridge the gap between 5,000-year-old Yogic knowledge and contemporary neuroscience. Our flagship PranaFlow device measures what no smartwatch can - precise nostril-specific airflow.

Our mission: Every practitioner deserves to know if their breathing practice is actually working. We make that possible through objective, measurable feedback.
