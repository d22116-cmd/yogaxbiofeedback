📚 Classical Swara Shastra Wisdom

▼
॥१०॥
चन्द्रनाडी प्रवाहेण सौम्यकार्याणि कारयेत् ।
When the Ida Nadi (left nostril) is flowing, one should perform gentle/peaceful activities.
॥११॥
सूर्यनाडी प्रवाहेण रौद्रकर्माणि कारयेत् ।
सुषुम्नाया प्रवाहेण भक्तिमोक्ष फलाप्ति च ।
When the Pingala Nadi (right nostril) is flowing, one should perform fierce/dynamic activities. When the Sushumna Nadi is flowing, one attains the fruits of devotion and liberation.

🌙 Swara (स्वर)

Ida Nadi (चन्द्र नाडी): Left nostril, lunar energy, cooling, Saumya Karma (gentle activities)

Pingala Nadi (सूर्य नाडी): Right nostril, solar energy, warming, Raudra Karma (dynamic activities)

Sushumna: Central channel, balanced state, optimal for Bhakti and Moksha

🔥 Pancha Tattva (पञ्चतत्त्व)

Akasha (आकाश): ≤0.5 Angula - Space element, subtle breath

Agni (अग्नि): ≤4 Angula - Fire element, short breath

Vayu (वायु): ≤8 Angula - Air element, medium breath

Prithvi (पृथ्वी): ≤12 Angula - Earth element, long breath

Jal (जल): ≤16 Angula - Water element, very long breath
